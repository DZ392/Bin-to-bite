About Bin to Bite

Every year, 2.5 billion tons of food go to waste globally. That's why we created Bin to Bite — our mission is simple: reduce food waste by helping you make the most of the ingredients already in your kitchen.

Upload a photo, let our AI identify your ingredients, and discover delicious recipes you can make right now. No more throwing away food that could become your next great meal.
